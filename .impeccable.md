## Design Context

### Users
People operating their own Nock Brain, including Command Center customers and
school members. They need to see remembered facts and decisions, inspect evidence,
and understand what recall would return without reading agent internals.

### Brand Personality
Calm, clear, trustworthy. The user chose a quiet, light workspace on 2026-09-26.
Accuracy and readable memory content matter more than decorative dashboards.

### Aesthetic Direction
A light editorial workspace: warm paper, near-black text, restrained forest-green
accents, precise rules and generous reading space. Use offline font stacks with a
serif title and readable sans-serif controls. No external assets or font requests.

### Design Principles
- Make the selected store, snapshot age and verification limits easy to see.
- Prioritize readable content and evidence over charts or decorative cards.
- Use labels as well as color for integrity and lifecycle states.
- Provide keyboard navigation, visible focus and narrow-screen layouts.
- Keep every action honest: this milestone inspects and previews memory.
